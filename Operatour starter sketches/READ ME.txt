Operatour starter sketches
==========================

Every folder in here is one sketch. Drop any of them onto the Operatour window
and it becomes a layer. Play music, and watch it move.

Try this first:

  1. Open Operatour and play some music into your microphone, or route your
     player's output to an input device.
  2. Drop glsl-bands onto the window.
  3. Drop glsl-raymarch on top of it, then drag its opacity bar.

The folders:

  glsl-bands      three bands of the music, as colour. The simplest one.
  glsl-raymarch   a heavy 3D scene, all in one shader. A stress test that
                  moves with the sound.
  glsl-shadertoy  written the way Shadertoy sketches are: if you have one,
                  it runs like this.
  glsl-legacy     written the way most older shaders on the internet are.
  glsl-points     six hundred points per pixel, driven by the spectrum.
  p5-points       a p5.js sketch, a field of points that hears the music.
  p5-rings        p5.js rings that breathe with the level and kick on the
                  beat.
  p5-3d           p5.js in 3D, spinning with the music.

Make your own: a folder with one sketch.glsl in it (a GLSL shader), or one
sketch.js that declares setup() or draw() (a p5.js sketch). Drop it on the
window the same way. The full story is at sialk.net/operatour/docs, and everything
your sketch can read from the music is at sialk.net/contract.
